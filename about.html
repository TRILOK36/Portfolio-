About me

Who I am, what I'm studying, and where I'm headed.

I'm a second-year B.Tech Computer Science (AI/ML) student at Sandip University, batch of 2025. Most of what I build starts as a coursework brief and turns into something I actually want to keep improving — a gas-leak alerting system that had to think in real time, a timetable optimizer that had to reason under constraints.

I'm drawn to problems where the interesting part isn't the UI, it's the logic underneath: search algorithms, sensor data, decision rules. My goal over the next couple of years is to get deep enough in applied ML and systems thinking to work on products where that logic actually matters.
